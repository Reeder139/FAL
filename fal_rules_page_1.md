# How It Works

## The basics

Fantasy Angling Leagues is a season-long competition for UK carp anglers. You catch real fish, log them in the app, and score points on weight. The more consistently you catch, the higher you climb.

Every fish must be caught by you, in UK waters, during the season it's entered in.

---

## Seasons

There are two leagues each year.

**Summer League** — 1 April to 30 September. Your best **5** fish count.

**Winter League** — 1 October to 31 March. Your best **3** fish count.

Only your best fish score. A better fish replaces your smallest counting one — nothing is ever lost, it just stops counting.

---

## Divisions

You're placed in one of three divisions when you join, based on your declared personal best.

| Division | Personal best |
|---|---|
| Division 1 | 40lb and over |
| Division 2 | 30lb to 40lb |
| Division 3 | Under 30lb |

You compete against anglers of a similar standard. Win your division and you're promoted for the next season; finish at the bottom and you're relegated.

**Declaring your PB.** If you don't verify your personal best, you start in Division 1. Proving a lower PB is what earns you a place in an easier division — so it's worth doing.

---

## Scoring

Points are awarded on weight, measured to the ounce. Bigger fish are worth disproportionately more, because they're disproportionately harder to catch.

Fish under 15lb don't score.

**Bonuses**
- Personal best: +5%
- A named, known fish: +5%

Your live points are shown the moment you enter a weight, so you always know where a fish puts you.

---

## Logging a catch

1. Photograph your fish. A normal trophy shot is fine — held, with the scales visible where you can.
2. Log it in the app: weight in pounds and ounces, the water, and when you caught it.
3. Choose who sees it. You can hide the venue and keep it off the public feed.

Log your catch within **7 days**. Sooner is better.

**Evidence.** The bigger the fish, the more we'll ask for. A routine twenty needs one photo. A fish that could win a division may need a second photo showing the flank, your original camera images, or the name of another member who was there. We'll ask if we need to — you don't have to do it up front.

---

## Membership

**Free.** Log your catches, build your record, follow other anglers, see what your fish would have scored.

**Member.** Everything above, plus you're in the league, on the table, and eligible for prizes.

**Joining mid-season.** You can join at any time. Fish you caught before you joined stay in your personal log but don't score — your season starts the day you become a member.

**Lapsed membership.** If your membership lapses, fish caught during the gap don't score, and you're no longer eligible for prizes that season. Points already banked stay on the table.

---

## Prizes

**Division winners** — £1,500 each, six across the year.

**Monthly competitions** — tackle bundles and vouchers.

**The Grand Final** — the six divisional winners fish 48 hours at a top UK water each spring. Winner takes **£20,000**.

Continuous membership across the season is required to be eligible.

---

## Fair play

Everyone here is on the honour system, and it works because anglers police it themselves.

- Your own fish, caught by you
- Accurate weights, on proper scales
- Photos from the actual capture, unedited
- One entry per fish

We check submissions automatically and members can report anything that looks wrong. Where a catch is questioned, we'll ask you first — you'll always get the chance to explain before anything is decided.

Deliberately false entries mean removal from the league and forfeiture of any prizes.

---

## Fish care

Nothing here is worth harming a fish for.

Get your photos quickly and get it back. If a shot means more time out of the water, don't take it — no points are worth it, and we'd rather you logged a fish with one hurried photo than a perfect set.

Unhooking mat, proper handling, minimum time on the bank. Any evidence of poor fish care will see a catch rejected regardless of its weight.
